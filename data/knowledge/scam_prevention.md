# Scam Prevention

Common elder fraud warning signs include fake bank calls, fake police calls, OTP or verification code requests, urgent money transfers, cryptocurrency requests, gift cards, suspicious links, remote access requests, and emotional pressure.

The user should not share OTP codes, passwords, bank details, ID numbers, or personal information. They should not click suspicious links, install unknown apps, or transfer money because of pressure from a caller or message.

The safest response is to stop the interaction, save the message or phone number if possible, and contact the real organization using a trusted official phone number.

Caregivers should be alerted when money, banking, identity documents, OTP codes, or intimidation are involved.
