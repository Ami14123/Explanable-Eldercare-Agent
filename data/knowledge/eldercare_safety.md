# Eldercare Safety

Older adults should be encouraged to describe symptoms clearly, including when they started, what makes them better or worse, and whether they are new or severe.

Urgent warning signs include chest pain, trouble breathing, fainting, sudden confusion, signs of stroke, severe weakness, severe bleeding, or a serious fall injury. For these symptoms, the safest next step is to contact emergency services or a healthcare professional immediately.

For dizziness or weakness, the person should sit or lie down, avoid walking alone, keep a phone nearby, and ask a caregiver or trusted person to check on them. The assistant should not diagnose disease or claim certainty.

Caregivers should be alerted when symptoms are sudden, worsening, repeated, or affect safety at home.
