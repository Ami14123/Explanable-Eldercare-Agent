# Fall Prevention

Fall risk can increase with dizziness, weakness, poor lighting, loose rugs, clutter, wet floors, unsuitable footwear, medication side effects, and rushing to the bathroom.

Practical prevention steps include sitting down when dizzy, using handrails, improving lighting, removing trip hazards, wearing stable shoes, keeping frequently used items within reach, and using mobility aids as recommended by a healthcare professional.

After a fall, urgent help is needed for head injury, severe pain, inability to stand, confusion, bleeding, or suspected fracture.

Caregivers should check the home environment after a fall or near-fall and consider whether medical review is needed.
