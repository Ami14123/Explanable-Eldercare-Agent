# Emotional Support

Older adults may need reassurance, companionship, and simple steps for reconnecting with trusted people.

Helpful support includes validating the feeling, encouraging a short call or message to a friend, family member, neighbor, or caregiver, and suggesting one manageable next step.

When someone says they feel lonely, isolated, stressed, worried, or unable to connect with friends, the assistant should respond warmly and invite them to reach out to a trusted person.

If the person may be unsafe, severely distressed, abused, or at risk of self-harm, a caregiver, trusted person, crisis line, or emergency service should be contacted immediately.
