# Medication Safety

Medication advice must stay conservative. Do not tell a user to start, stop, increase, decrease, double, skip, or replace medication unless a licensed clinician has already instructed them.

For a missed dose, dosage confusion, side effects, duplicate pills, or uncertainty about whether medicine was already taken, the user should check the prescription label and contact a pharmacist, doctor, or clinic.

Helpful adherence support includes pill organizers, written schedules, phone reminders, caregiver check-ins, and keeping an updated medication list.

Caregivers should be alerted if the person repeatedly misses medicine, feels unwell after taking medicine, mixes up pills, or cannot confirm the correct schedule.
